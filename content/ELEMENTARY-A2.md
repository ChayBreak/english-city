# Elementary English A2 · Units 1–6

The 24 English City lessons follow the first six topic and grammar areas of Macmillan Education’s [American Language Hub, Second Edition, Level 1](https://www.macmillanenglish.com/us/catalogue/courses/adults-and-young-adults/american-language-hub-second-edition/levels-samples), print Student’s Book ISBN 9781035197767. The syllabus is the publisher’s **ALH2_SB_L1_Scope_and_Sequence_spread** download on that page. English City wrote its own lessons, dialogues, practice questions and writing tasks.

| Unit | English City content | Writing task |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Meeting people | Introductions, family relationships, appearance and polite requests | An email to a new classmate |
| 2. A day in your life | Routines, frequency, preferences and making suggestions | A post about an ideal Saturday |
| 3. Work and study | Job interviews, requirements, question words and phone messages | A reply recommending an evening course |
| 4. Homes and neighborhoods | Rooms, things to do nearby, landmarks and directions | Directions and a neighborhood description |
| 5. Trips and travel stories | Transport, past questions and answers, hotel check-in | A short travel story |
| 6. Food and meals | Count and noncount nouns, quantities, recipes and ordering | A restaurant review |

Each unit has three 25-minute lessons and one 30-minute review with a quiz and a writing assignment. Lesson 1.1 is public. The main explanations and instructions are in Kazakh, Russian and English. Examples and exercises use English. Speaking activities use the platform’s browser audio and recording features.

The demo inserts the new course on upgrade while retaining existing accounts, payments and progress. On a hosted Supabase project, apply the additive `20261122135336_elementary_a2_units_1_6.sql` migration. The existing subscription rules apply. To update the source seed after curriculum edits, run `npm run seed:sql`.
